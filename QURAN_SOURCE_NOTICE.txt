The package's quran.sty identifies its Arabic surah names as taken from:
http://tanzil.net/res/text/metadata/quran-data.xml

This app's Quran reader displays the extracted ayah text as supplied by the
package. It does not provide an independent textual collation against a
fresh Tanzil download. The Quran text itself is not editable inside the app.
